## Decision protocol

1. Search the blueprint and this log. Reuse an existing answer and cite it.
2. Apply the intent and ranked tiebreakers. For a supported, reversible choice,
   decide, log it as provisional, and continue.
3. Ask only for a genuinely new product commitment that is costly to reverse,
   or a material contradiction that the record cannot resolve.
4. Describe what a person would observe. Recommend an option with its rationale.
   Silence may permit a reversible provisional choice, never an irreversible commitment.
5. Continue independent work while a required answer is pending.
